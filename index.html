At a glance

Which GI is WindyGI closest to?

WindyGI was not built to copy one engine. It started as a port of kajiya to UE 4.27 and was then reshaped after the real-time GI that shipped in games. As it stands now:

WindyGI Hardware is closest to id Tech 8 (DOOM: The Dark Ages)

Cache first: a world radiance cache in a spatial hash, cascaded irradiance probe volumes with a depth test against leaks, ray tracing spent on keeping those caches current, work on the async queue. The cache layers came from kajiya and from DDGI. What id Tech 8 has and WindyGI does not: a ray per pixel into those caches (WindyGI's pixels read the caches directly), and separate visibility and shading passes. At Low and Medium WindyGI is the probe volume alone, which is DDGI (RTXGI) read the way Lumen reads its irradiance field.

WindyGI Software is closest to Lumen, software, Medium

Lumen's surface cache brought to UE 4.27 (mesh cards, relighting, radiosity), rays that march signed distance fields, and the irradiance field Lumen uses at Medium as the final gather. It keeps that gather at every tier instead of switching to screen probes at High and Epic, traces only the global distance field and adds caches of its own.

WindyGI and Lumen: what is alike, what differs

WindyGI is not a port of Lumen. It solves the same problem in UE 4.27, borrows some of Lumen's building blocks and replaces others. In short:

  • Same job. Fully dynamic diffuse global illumination with multiple bounces, sky light and emissive surfaces, nothing baked.
  • Same tiers. Low, Medium, High and Epic come from sg.GlobalIlluminationQuality, as in UE 5. Lumen Low has no GI; WindyGI Low keeps a cheap one.
  • Same short-range AO. A depth-buffer horizon search, as Lumen's.
  • Async compute on both, where the GPU supports it.
  • Reflections are not part of the comparison. Both sides use screen-space reflections on the sheets; Lumen reflections are off.
